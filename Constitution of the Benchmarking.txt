Dataset:
Split in 70:15:15 
For training, we do augmentation for ALL samples in training.  More specifically 
Transforms: view v = 4·flip + k, where flip is 0 or 1 (mirror left–right) and k is 0–3 (then rotate by k·90°). That gives 8 views: 4 rotations, each with and without a mirror. These are all the symmetries of a square.
For the smudges dataset we limit to R_e<10" (mainly because AION is 96x96)

Description of LSBG Dataset:
Holds Legacy Survey DR10 cutouts of 59,819 DES LSB galaxies. The main dataset is fluxes, a float32 array of shape (59819, 4, 256, 256): bands g, r, i, z, each 256 × 256 pixels at 0.262″/px, with zero point 22.5.
Three 1-D datasets sit alongside it, one entry per galaxy: ra and dec (degrees), and label (int64, 1 for every galaxy, so it carries no information). The file attributes record bands = g, r, i, z, image_size = 256, pixel_scale = 0.262 and survey = "Legacy Survey ls-dr10".
The LSBG project uses only 9,758 of these galaxies, taking bands g, r, i and the central 96 × 96 pixels (for AION. How do we make the cutout size consistent across models ? Can we? ).
